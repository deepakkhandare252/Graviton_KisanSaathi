# Karnataka Scheme Rules Verification Reference

This document catalogs the verified policy rules, government gazette guidelines, and official verification mechanisms for the 8 featured Karnataka agriculture and farmer welfare schemes.

---

## 1. PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi & Karnataka Raitha Siri
- **Official Portals**: https://fruits.karnataka.gov.in / https://pmkisan.gov.in
- **Governing Body**: Department of Agriculture, Govt. of Karnataka & MoA&FW, GoI
- **Financial Architecture**: ₹6,000 (Central DBT via PFMS in 3 tranches of ₹2,000) + ₹4,000 (Karnataka e-Kshana State Top-up). Total: ₹10,000/year.
- **Authoritative Data Sources**:
  - Land ownership: Karnataka Bhoomi Portal RTC database.
  - Farmer Identity: FRUITS (Farmer Registration & Unified Beneficiary Information System) FID.
  - Payment Routing: NPCI Aadhaar Payments Bridge (APB).
- **Verified Disqualifications**:
  - Any family member paying income tax in the previous assessment year.
  - Former or present MPs, MLAs, MLCs, Mayors, Zilla Panchayat Presidents.
  - Regular employees of Central/State Govt/PSUs (excluding Class-IV/Group D).
  - Registered institutional landholders.

## 2. Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (Karnataka Samrakshane)
- **Official Portal**: https://samrakshane.karnataka.gov.in
- **Governing Body**: Department of Agriculture, Govt. of Karnataka
- **Insurance Unit**: Notified Gram Panchayat / Hobli level for notified crops.
- **Premium Structure**:
  - Kharif food & oilseed crops: 2.0%
  - Rabi food & oilseed crops: 1.5%
  - Commercial / Horticultural crops: 5.0%
- **Verification Trigger**: Must match Karnataka Bele Sameekshe (Farmer Mobile Crop Survey) geo-tagged crop record.

---

## 3. PMKSY — Drip & Sprinkler Micro Irrigation Subsidy
- **Official Portal**: https://horticulturedir.karnataka.gov.in (DIMS Portal)
- **Subsidy Matrix**:
  - SC/ST Small & Marginal Farmers: 90% subsidy up to 5 acres.
  - General / OBC Small & Marginal Farmers: 75% subsidy up to 5 acres.
  - Other farmers: 45% to 55% subsidy up to 12.5 acres.
- **Mandatory Requirements**: Perennial water source (borewell, open well, or farm pond) with functional pump set and RTC record.

---

## 4. Ganga Kalyana Scheme
- **Official Portals**: https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in / https://kmdc.karnataka.gov.in
- **Target Groups**: SC, ST, OBC (Cat-1, 2A, 2B, 3A, 3B), and Religious Minorities.
- **Eligibility Boundaries**:
  - Landholding: 1.5 to 5.0 acres of unirrigated land.
  - Income Ceiling: Family annual income ≤ ₹2,00,000 (Rural).
  - Exclusion: Strict exclusion if land already possesses an operational energized borewell.
- **Verification Mechanism**: Nadakacheri RD-numbered Caste & Income Certificate + VAO Mahazar.

---

## 5. Krishi Yanthradhare Custom Hiring Centers (CHSC)
- **Operator**: Managed through private-public partnership (SKDRDP) under Department of Agriculture.
- **Tariff Structure**: 30% to 50% below prevailing market machine rental rates.
- **Eligibility**: Open to all registered cultivators with FRUITS ID. Small and marginal farmers receive prioritized dispatch.

---

## 6. Chief Minister's Raitha Vidya Nidhi
- **Portal**: https://ssp.postmatric.karnataka.gov.in
- **Annual Benefit**:
  - ITI / PUC / Diploma: ₹2,500 (Boys), ₹3,000 (Girls)
  - Degree / Post-Graduation: ₹5,000 (Boys), ₹5,500 (Girls)
  - Professional / Medical / Engineering: ₹10,000 (Boys), ₹11,000 (Girls)
- **Automatic Matching**: Direct database cross-reference between Bhoomi-FRUITS ID and student State Scholarship Portal (SSP) registration.

---

## 7. Raitha Sanjeevini Accidental Relief Scheme
- **Administering Authority**: Karnataka State Agricultural Marketing Board (KSAMB).
- **Accidental Cover**: ₹2,00,000 for fatal accidents, ₹1,00,000 for permanent disability.
- **Coverage**: Snakebites, farm machinery accidents, pesticide poisoning, APMC transit accidents.
- **Target Group**: Karnataka resident farmers and landless agricultural laborers aged 18 to 65.

---

## 8. Yashaswini Health Insurance Scheme for Rural Cooperative Farmers
- **Governing Body**: Department of Cooperation, Govt. of Karnataka.
- **Coverage Limit**: Cashless surgical and in-patient cover up to ₹5,00,000 per family per year in 570+ empanelled hospitals.
- **Eligibility Requirement**: Minimum 3 months active membership in a Primary Agricultural Credit Cooperative Society (PACS) or Milk Producers Union.
